04 / FAQ

Questions, answered

The things most people ask before we start — pricing, timelines, ownership, and what happens after launch.

  • WSN helps tradespeople, consultants, salons, clinics and other UK local businesses launch websites that look credible and generate more engagement.

  • Every site is quoted to the brief, so there's no fixed price list — you'll know the full cost before we start, with no hourly meter. Most local-business sites land in a clear range we'll confirm on our first call.

  • My builds usually take around a week from start to finish. I work with just one client at a time, giving your project full attention and zero distractions. We set a clear timeline together, and you'll know exactly what's happening at every stage.

  • Completely. The site, the domain, the content and the logins are all yours — built on tools you can keep and handed over in full at launch. No lock-in.

  • On Business and Premium builds, yes — we set up a simple CMS and show you how to edit text, images and pages. Prefer us to handle it? That's what the Care Plan is for.

  • That's the Care Plan: hosting, security updates, uptime monitoring and small edits, so your site stays healthy long after go-live. It's optional, and the natural next step from a one-off build.
