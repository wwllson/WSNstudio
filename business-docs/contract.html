7 Extra work & changes of scope

If the Client asks for work beyond the agreed scope, WSN will quote it before starting. No extra work is carried out — and no extra charge is made — without the Client’s written go-ahead (email is fine).

8 Ownership

Once all invoices are paid in full, ownership of the final website design and the custom files created for the Project transfers to the Client. Until then, they remain WSN’s property.

This does not include third-party components (themes, plugins, stock assets, fonts, hosting), which stay under their own licences, or WSN’s own tools and underlying know-how. WSN may show the finished work in its portfolio and marketing unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

9 Third-party services

Costs for hosting, domain names, premium plugins, stock imagery, email services and similar are the Client’s responsibility and are not part of the project price unless stated in clause 1.

10 Cancellation

Either side may end this agreement in writing. If the Client cancels after work has started, the deposit is not refunded and any work done beyond the deposit is invoiced as a fair proportion of the project quote, reflecting the stage reached. If WSN cancels, the Client is refunded for any paid work not yet delivered.

13 Confidentiality

Each side will keep the other’s private business information confidential and use it only to deliver the Project.
